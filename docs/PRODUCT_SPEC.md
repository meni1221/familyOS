# Shlanu — Product Specification

## Product Name
**Shlanu**

## Category
Family life management SaaS

## Primary Audience
Couples, parents, and families managing shared responsibilities.

## Initial Market
Israel, with internationalization built into the architecture.

## Product Promise
"Less mental load. More time for what matters."

Hebrew: "כל מה שהמשפחה צריכה, במקום אחד."

## Core Value Proposition
Shlanu helps families coordinate daily tasks, schedules, baby routines, household spending, documents, and reminders through a shared, secure workspace.

## Product Principles
1. Family comes first. Minimize cognitive load.
2. Important actions accessible within one or two interactions.
3. Mobile experience is first-class.
4. Design for real empty, loading, error, success, and offline states.
5. Data ownership and privacy are explicit.
6. Never invent data or fabricate successful operations.
7. Every visible interactive element has a defined behavior.
8. Prefer simple, maintainable architecture.
9. Build a complete, usable MVP before expanding.
10. Accessibility, performance, and security are product requirements.

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Parents managing household tasks, schedules, and children
- **Secondary**: Couples coordinating shared responsibilities
- **Tertiary**: Extended family members with limited access

## Core Features (MVP)
1. Authentication & onboarding
2. Family workspace management
3. Task management
4. Family calendar
5. Baby tracker (optional)
6. Family budget
7. Document vault
8. In-app notifications
9. Settings & member management

- Default language: Hebrew (RTL)

## Success Criteria
- A family can manage daily tasks effectively
- Calendar coordination works for all members
- Baby tracking is simple and accurate
- Budget tracking is reliable
- Documents are secure and accessible
- The interface feels premium and trustworthy
